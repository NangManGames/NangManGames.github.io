Studio

NangmanGames is a one-person studio in South Korea. For years I was the classic “ideas guy”, all daydreams and nothing finished. These days I’m pulling those daydreams out one at a time and actually putting them together.

Dock 7 is where they’ve ended up: a station circling the moon, ships nobody came back for, a radio left on at night, and the things people left behind.

Nangman (낭만) is a Korean word for a warm, slightly wistful kind of romance.
